The Man Behind the Fire:

Aidan Ingram

Aidan has had a passion for storytelling for as long as he can remember. As a young kid he learned to love film through creative masterpieces such as The Lord of the Rings, Star Wars, Christopher Nolan films like Interstellar, and many more.

Aidan believes that every story has the substance and the potential to be as compelling as those told through feature length films across Hollywood. All they need is someone who can bring light to those stories in a way that no one can do with an AI prompt or a smartphone camera.
